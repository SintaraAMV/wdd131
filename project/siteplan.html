Temple Paths

Site plan for the WDD 131 final project

Site Name

Temple Paths. This name was chosen because the site is a travel guide, not a photo album. "Paths" points to planning a visit, and the name works for temples in any country, which matches the worldwide subject declared in Week 04.

Site Purpose

Temple Paths helps a Latter-day Saint traveler choose a temple to visit. The site will provide a short welcome, featured temples from several continents, a filterable list with country, dedication year, size, and photo, and a visit planner that saves a temple, date, and note in the browser.

  • Home: purpose of the guide and three featured temples.
  • Temples: cards built from an array, with filters for all, old, new, and region.
  • Plan a Visit: a form that saves the plan in localStorage and lists saved plans.

Scenarios

These questions come from the target visitor: a member planning a temple trip.

  • Which temples were dedicated after 2000?
  • How do I save a temple I want to visit later?
  • Which temples are in South America?

  • Navy, #102a43: header, footer, and headings.
  • Paper, #f7f3ec: page background.
